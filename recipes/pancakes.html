Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 Egg
  • Flour plain 200g
  • Pinch of salt
  • Milk or water (1 pint in total but dont use all)

Method

  1. Sieve flour into bit bowl and salt
  2. Make a dip in middle and add egg into dip
  3. Slowly mix egg in flour
  4. When fully mixed, add milk and water while stirring, should have thick consistency, hand blender to get rid of lumps
  5. Refridgerate for 15 mins
