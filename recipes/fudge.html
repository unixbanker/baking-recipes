Fudge

Instructions

  • Butter 150g
  • Evaporated Milk 410g
  • Caster Sugar 500g
  • Vanilla Essence 2 tsps

Method

  1. Pour evaporated milk into medium pot
  2. Dump butter in
  3. Dump caster sugar in
  4. Put ring on high heat and bring to boil
  5. Stir constantly for 20 mins until it gets thick, take it off the heat and whisk for 10 minutes till texture goes sticky
  6. Add vanilla essence when thick
  7. Whisk another 5 minutes, grease proof paper and flatten out, leave for 2 and a half hours to set and store in fridge
